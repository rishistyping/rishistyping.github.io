# Works

Public credits for Rishabh Chakrabarty.

## As artist of record

### For All Mankind (2023–2024)

Immersive installation using a stereo-3D generative diffusion model trained on Martian terrain, shown with holographic glasses and four-dimensional spatial sound. Sound design: William Russell. Additional credits: NASA / JPL-Caltech / UArizona.

- Bright Festival, Florence, 2024: https://www.brightfestival.com/art_event/for-all-mankind-rishabh-chakrabarty/
- NeurIPS 2024 Creative AI, author of record: https://neurips.cc/virtual/2024/105442

### For All Mankind: New Worlds (December 2024)

Later version shown at Beyond Basel during Art Basel Miami 2024. Same stereo-3D model, framed around Miami as a historic launchpad for spaceflight.

### EnPerspectiva (2024)

Large-scale light and spatial-sound installation at Bright Festival CONNECT / Kunstkraftwerk Leipzig. Public credit line: Rish × LedPulse × Omni Soundlab × Symbiotism. Built during a six-month artist residency (volumetric LED, Dolby Atmos, visitor-phone data).

- https://connect.brightfestival.com/art/enperspectiva/
