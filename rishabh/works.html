Works

Public credits as they stand. Where a page names a studio, that is the credit.

As artist of record

  • For All Mankind (2023–2024). Immersive installation. Participants become interplanetary explorers walking Martian terrain generated by a stereo-3D generative diffusion model trained on extra-terrestrial imagery, shown with holographic glasses and four-dimensional spatial sound. Sound design: William Russell. Additional credits: NASA / JPL-Caltech / UArizona. Bright Festival, Florence, 2024, visited by several thousand people. Also a NeurIPS 2024 Creative AI artwork, author of record: Rishabh Chakrabarty.
  • For All Mankind: New Worlds (December 2024). Later version shown at Beyond Basel during Art Basel Miami 2024. Same stereo-3D model, framed around Miami as a historic launchpad for spaceflight. Trained on a large Martian terrain corpus; the Miami showing engaged tens of thousands of visitors.
  • EnPerspectiva (2024). Large-scale light and spatial-sound installation at Bright Festival CONNECT / Kunstkraftwerk Leipzig, the first permanent museum in Germany dedicated to digital and multimedia arts. Public credit line: Rish × LedPulse × Omni Soundlab × Symbiotism (surname missing on that line). Built from a six-month artist residency: more than 100,000 volumetric LED elements, Dolby Atmos 3D spatial audio, and real-time data from visitors’ phones. Page.

Refik Anadol Studio (named in production credits)

These works are credited to Refik Anadol Studio. I worked on them as a generative designer / ML and NeuroAI researcher: dataset preparation, generative pipelines, latent-space tools, and production support. I am not the lead artist.

  • Unsupervised — Machine Hallucinations, Museum of Modern Art, New York (2022). StyleGAN2 trained on 138,151 records from MoMA’s collection; 24×24 ft data sculpture; MoMA’s first generative AI work in the collection. MoMA; RAS.
  • Sense of Space / Molecular Architecture, 17th Venice Architecture Biennale (2021). Immersive 3D-printed AI data sculpture from ~70 TB of Human Connectome Project MRI data. I led the generative pipeline and ran HCP workflows on UCLA compute. RAS.
  • Architecting the Metaverse / Meta-Horizons: The Future Now, DDP Design Museum, Seoul (2022). Zaha Hadid Architects × Refik Anadol Studio × OpenAI. Infinity Room with multimodal AI over ZHA’s archive; among the earlier large artistic uses of DALL·E 2. I led the six-month collaboration with Patrik Schumacher’s office. ZHA; RAS.
  • Machine Hallucinations — Nature Dreams, König Galerie, Berlin (2021–2022). GAN-based data sculpture from a very large public nature-image corpus; also Winds of Berlin on the St. Agnes tower. Later shown at the Hammer Museum and Centre Pompidou-Metz. RAS.
  • Renaissance Dreams, Palazzo Strozzi. RAS.
  • Machine Hallucinations — Space, Sotheby’s NFT collection. Two million+ images from ISS, Hubble, and MRO; studio collaboration with NASA JPL dating to 2018. RAS.
  • Sense of Healing (2022). RAS × MindMaze Labs × MYND. Neuro-therapeutic data sculpture from brain data. Premiered at Aurora Institute, St. Tropez; later auctioned at the LuisaViaRoma × UNICEF Summer Gala in Capri. Note.
