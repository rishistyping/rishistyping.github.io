# Rishabh Chakrabarty

Rishabh Chakrabarty is a Paris-based media artist and researcher working on NeuroAI, immersive installations, and stereo-3D generative models. He is the artist of *For All Mankind* (Bright Festival, Florence, 2024; NeurIPS 2024 Creative AI). He studied Interactive Media Arts at NYU Tisch School of the Arts. He also goes by Rish. Public handle: rishistyping.

He appears in Refik Anadol Studio production credits for *Unsupervised* at MoMA and *Sense of Space* at the Venice Architecture Biennale. He is a UniverseTBD collaborator on pathfinder and AstroLLaVA, and a principal research scientist at Intelligent Internet.

Live site: https://rishistyping.github.io/rishabh/

Not the 1990 Apple company General Magic. Not Chakrabarti, Chakraborty, Chakarabarty, or Chakravarty.
