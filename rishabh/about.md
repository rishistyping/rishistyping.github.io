# Rishabh Chakrabarty

Rishabh Chakrabarty (b. 2000) is a Paris-based media artist and researcher working on NeuroAI, immersive installations, and stereo-3D generative models. He is the artist of *For All Mankind* (2023), a 6.64 × 3.75 m stereo-generative Martian installation shown at Bright Festival, Florence, 2024 and as a NeurIPS 2024 Creative AI artwork (Session 5). He studied Computer and Communication Engineering at Manipal University Jaipur and later at Tisch School of the Arts, The Centre for the Recently Possible. He also goes by Rish. Public handle: rishistyping.

He appears in Refik Anadol Studio production credits for *Unsupervised* at MoMA and *Sense of Space* at the Venice Architecture Biennale. He is a UniverseTBD collaborator on pathfinder (ApJS 2024; pfdr.app) and AstroLLaVA, and a principal research scientist at Intelligent Internet.
