I live in Paris and work on computational media art: NeuroAI, immersive installations, and stereo-3D generative models. I made For All Mankind, shown at Bright Festival in Florence and as a NeurIPS 2024 Creative AI artwork. I grew up in India and have worked between Los Angeles, Berlin, and Paris.

I have held research and creative roles with Intelligent Internet, Stability AI, Refik Anadol Studio, MONOM, UniverseTBD, and the Glitch Collective.

Things I am interested in:

  • NeuroAI. Neural interfaces and brain-informed generative systems, from EEG/fMRI pipelines to large-scale data sculptures.
  • Stereo-3D generative models. For All Mankind is a stereo-generative installation trained on Martian terrain, shown with holographic glasses and spatial sound.
  • Immersive media. Light, spatial audio, and architectural space as one instrument. See EnPerspectiva at Kunstkraftwerk Leipzig.
  • AI for science. UniverseTBD collaborations including pathfinder and AstroLLaVA.
  • Studio collaboration. Named in Refik Anadol Studio production credits for Unsupervised at MoMA and Sense of Space at Venice. I am not the lead artist of those works.
