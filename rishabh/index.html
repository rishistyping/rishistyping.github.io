I live in Paris and work between computational media art and applied research: NeuroAI, immersive installations, and stereo-3D generative models. I grew up in India and have worked in Los Angeles, Berlin, New York, and Paris. I studied at Tisch School of the Arts, The Centre for the Recently Possible. I also go by Rish. Handle: rishistyping.

I made For All Mankind, an immersive installation using a stereo-3D generative model trained on Martian terrain, shown with holographic glasses and spatial sound. It was at Bright Festival in Florence in 2024 (sound design by William Russell) and listed as a NeurIPS 2024 Creative AI artwork under my name. A later version, For All Mankind: New Worlds, was shown at Beyond Basel during Art Basel Miami 2024.

At Refik Anadol Studio (2020–2023) I worked as a machine-learning and NeuroAI researcher on the Machine Hallucinations program. I am named in production credits for Unsupervised at MoMA, Sense of Space at the Venice Architecture Biennale, Architecting the Metaverse with Zaha Hadid Architects, and Nature Dreams at König Galerie. Those works are credited to the studio. I am not the lead artist.

I have also held research and creative roles with Intelligent Internet, Stability AI, MONOM in Berlin, UniverseTBD, the Glitch Collective at Château du Feÿ, Kunstkraftwerk Leipzig, UCLA’s Semel Institute, and Orthogonal Research and Education Laboratory.

Things I am interested in:

  • NeuroAI and neural interfaces. EEG, EMG, fMRI, and fNIRS pipelines that connect brain and biosensing data to real-time audiovisual environments. I founded the neural-interface research thread inside RAS Media Lab, and I have worked with Dr. Taylor Kuhn’s lab at UCLA on neuroimaging analysis.
  • Stereo-3D generative models. For All Mankind is built around a stereo-generative diffusion model trained on Martian surface pairs, producing synthetic 3D anaglyph views shown with holographic glasses and four-dimensional spatial sound.
  • Immersive media as one instrument. Light, spatial audio, and architectural space. See EnPerspectiva at Kunstkraftwerk Leipzig: volumetric LED, Dolby Atmos, and visitor-phone data as a single perceptual field.
  • AI for science. UniverseTBD collaborations including pathfinder (semantic literature search over 350,000+ ADS papers; ApJS 2024), AstroLLaVA, and a 2025 survey on LLM hypothesis generation.
  • Open-source AI systems. At Intelligent Internet I work on fully open-source models, agents, datasets, and full-stack applications meant to be owned and controlled by the people who use them.
  • Embodied and developmental models. With Orthogonal Research (RBP / meta-brain models) I work on continual developmental neurosimulation and developmental Braitenberg vehicles. See the JOPRO page.
