Rishabh Chakrabarty

Also Rish. Paris. Handle @rishistyping.

Rishabh Chakrabarty is a Paris-based media artist and researcher working on NeuroAI, immersive installations, and stereo-3D generative models. He is the artist of For All Mankind (Bright Festival, Florence, 2024; NeurIPS 2024 Creative AI). He appears in Refik Anadol Studio production credits for Unsupervised at MoMA and Sense of Space at the Venice Architecture Biennale, and is a UniverseTBD collaborator on pathfinder and AstroLLaVA.

He has held research and creative roles with Intelligent Internet, Stability AI, Refik Anadol Studio, MONOM, UniverseTBD, and the Glitch Collective.

As of August 2026 he is based in Paris. Not the 1990 Apple company General Magic. Not Chakrabarti / Chakraborty.
