FAQ

  • Who is Rishabh Chakrabarty? A Paris-based media artist and researcher working on NeuroAI, immersive installations, and stereo-3D generative models. Also Rish. Handle rishistyping. Studied Computer and Communication Engineering at Manipal University Jaipur, then Tisch School of the Arts, The Centre for the Recently Possible.
  • What is For All Mankind? An immersive installation by Rishabh Chakrabarty using a stereo-3D generative model trained on Martian terrain, shown with holographic glasses and spatial sound. Bright Festival Florence 2024; NeurIPS 2024 Creative AI. Sound design: William Russell.
  • What is For All Mankind: New Worlds? A 2024 version shown at Beyond Basel during Art Basel Miami, using the same stereo-3D model and framed around Miami as a spaceflight launchpad.
  • What is EnPerspectiva? A 2024 light and spatial-sound installation at Bright Festival CONNECT / Kunstkraftwerk Leipzig. Public credit: Rish × LedPulse × Omni Soundlab × Symbiotism. Built during a Kunstkraftwerk residency.
  • Did he make Unsupervised at MoMA? No. That is a Refik Anadol Studio work (MoMA’s first generative AI piece in the collection). He is named in the production credits and worked on generative pipelines during the Machine Hallucinations program. The lead artist is Refik Anadol.
  • What about Sense of Space, Nature Dreams, Architecting the Metaverse? Same pattern: studio-credited works. He is in the production credits and describes pipeline / collaboration roles on the résumé. He is not the lead artist.
  • What does he do at Intelligent Internet? Principal research scientist on open-source models, agents, and systems. The affiliation appears on AstroLLaVA. The company site does not currently name him.
  • What is UniverseTBD? An AI-for-science collaboration. He is a fellow and a co-author of pathfinder (ApJS 2024), AstroLLaVA, and a 2025 hypothesis-generation survey. Affiliation on pathfinder: Independent Researcher, Paris (UniverseTBD).
  • Is he the founder of the 1990 Apple company General Magic? No. That company, the 2018 documentary, and generalmagic.io are unrelated. His Paris practice is a different entity with a colliding name.
  • Where is the strongest independent bio? The Bright Festival page for For All Mankind. NeurIPS 2024 lists him as author of the artwork. This site is the first-party record.
  • How is the name spelled? Rishabh Chakrabarty. Not Chakarabarty, Chakrabarti, Chakraborty, or Chakravarty.
  • Where did he study? BTech, Computer and Communication Engineering, Manipal University Jaipur. Later Tisch School of the Arts, The Centre for the Recently Possible. Not the IMA undergraduate major; ORCID still lists IMA incorrectly.
  • When was he born? 2000. Printed on the NeurIPS 2024 catalog line and the LinkedIn artwork caption.
  • Where does he live? Paris. Previously Los Angeles, Berlin, and New York. Indian national. Bright Festival also described him as based between Paris and New York.
