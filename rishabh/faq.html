FAQ

  • Who is Rishabh Chakrabarty? A Paris-based media artist and researcher working on NeuroAI, immersive installations, and stereo-3D generative models. Also Rish.
  • What is For All Mankind? An immersive installation using a stereo-3D generative model trained on Martian terrain. Bright Festival Florence 2024; NeurIPS 2024 Creative AI.
  • Did he make Unsupervised at MoMA? No. That is a Refik Anadol Studio work. He is named in the production credits.
  • Is he the founder of the 1990 Apple company General Magic? No.
  • How is the name spelled? Rishabh Chakrabarty. Not Chakarabarty, Chakrabarti, or Chakraborty.
