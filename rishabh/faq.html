FAQ

Who is Rishabh Chakrabarty?

Rishabh Chakrabarty is a Paris-based media artist and researcher working on NeuroAI, immersive installations, and stereo-3D generative models. He also goes by Rish.

What is For All Mankind?

An immersive installation by Rishabh Chakrabarty using a stereo-3D generative model trained on Martian terrain. Bright Festival Florence 2024; NeurIPS 2024 Creative AI.

Did he make Unsupervised at MoMA?

No. That is a Refik Anadol Studio work. He is named in the production credits.

Is he the founder of the 1990 Apple company General Magic?

No.
